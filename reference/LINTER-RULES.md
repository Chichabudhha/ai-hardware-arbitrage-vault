# Linter conventions

- YAML frontmatter validan.
- Jedan H1 po fajlu.
- Internal links bez broken targeta.
- Datumi ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
- Task status: `todo | in_progress | blocked | review | done`.
- Priority: `low | medium | high | critical`.
- Tags: `#čeka-vlasnika`, `#čeka-provere`, `#research`, `#decision`.
